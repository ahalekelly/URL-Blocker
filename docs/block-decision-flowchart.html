Passes. Every page a tab is allowed onto, whether or not its site is restricted, gives that tab a pass for the page. The pass lasts until the tab moves to another allowed page or closes, and survives the browser idling the extension. A browser restart clears it.

Outside links. A referrer from another site counts as outside. Some links arrive with no referrer: Chromium tells them apart from typed visits by the navigation’s transition (link, opened from another app, or form submit), while Safari counts every empty referrer as outside, because iOS apps open links without one. Before the page reports its referrer, only feeds and the hard schedule can block.

Single short videos. With short video hiding on, a link to one short video opens as a single video: YouTube /shorts/<id> redirects to /watch?v=<id> and Instagram /reel/<code> to /p/<code>. The destination runs through these steps first with the same arrival, and the tab gets a pass for it. A Facebook /reel/<id> page stays put with swiping disabled. A single short video is never a feed.

Android apps count as their site’s home page opened directly, checked for as long as they are on screen.
